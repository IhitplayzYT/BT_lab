Terms & Conditions

Fixed Deposit Terms and Conditions

1. Eligibility

  • Applicant must be a resident Indian citizen aged 18 years or above.
  • Valid PAN card is mandatory for all FD applications.
  • Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) may apply subject to RBI regulations.
  • Senior citizens (60+ years) are eligible for additional interest rate benefits.

2. Deposit Amount and Tenure

  • Minimum deposit amount: ₹10,000
  • Maximum deposit amount: ₹1,00,00,000 (1 crore)
  • Tenure range: 6 months to 120 months (10 years)
  • Tax Saver FD has a minimum tenure of 5 years with lock-in period.

3. Interest Rates

  • Interest rates are subject to change and determined by the bank's policy.
  • Interest is calculated quarterly and compounded as per the selected product.
  • Senior citizens receive an additional 0.50% interest rate benefit.
  • Women's FD products receive an additional 0.30% interest rate benefit.

4. Premature Withdrawal

  • Premature withdrawal is allowed subject to penalty charges.
  • Penalty of 1.0% will be levied on the applicable interest rate.
  • Tax Saver FDs cannot be withdrawn before the 5-year lock-in period.
  • Partial withdrawal is not permitted for standard FD products.

5. Taxation

  • Interest earned on FD is taxable as per the Income Tax Act.
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) will be deducted if interest exceeds ₹40,000 per year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
  • Form 15G/15H can be submitted to avoid TDS if eligible.
  • Tax Saver FD offers tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh.

6. Nomination

  • Nomination facility is available for all FD accounts.
  • Nominee details can be updated during the account tenure.
  • In case of the depositor's demise, the amount will be transferred to the nominee.

7. Maturity

  • On maturity, the principal and accrued interest will be credited to the linked bank account.
  • If no instructions are given, the FD will be automatically renewed for the same tenure.
  • Maturity instructions can be given up to 7 days before the maturity date.

8. Account Operations

  • FD account cannot be used for transaction purposes like a savings account.
  • No overdraft facility is available against FD (unless specifically opted for).
  • Loan against FD is available up to 90% of the deposit amount.

9. KYC Requirements

  • Complete KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is mandatory.
  • Documents required: Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport size photograph.
  • Additional documents may be required for high-value deposits (above ₹50 lakh).

10. Dispute Resolution

  • All disputes will be subject to the jurisdiction of Mumbai courts.
  • Customers can approach the Banking Ombudsman for unresolved complaints.
  • Grievance redressal mechanism is available within 30 working days.

11. Data Privacy

  • Customer information will be handled as per the bank's privacy policy.
  • Data will not be shared with third parties without customer consent.
  • All communications will be encrypted and secured.

12. Modifications

  • The bank reserves the right to modify these terms with prior notice.
  • Changes will be communicated via email and SMS.
  • Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of modified terms.
