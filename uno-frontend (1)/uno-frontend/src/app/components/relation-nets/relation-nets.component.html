Relationship risk review

Heuristic review priority from nominee nodes and directed nominee edges in this graph.

A review signal is not evidence of wrongdoing or an automated decision. Possible chains use exact nominee/customer name matches; identities are not verified.
How the heuristic score is calculated

Three or more directed links in a nominee chain: +30 (two links: +15); a directed cycle: +35; a nominee linked from multiple customers: +15 per shared nominee, capped at +30; groups of five or more customers: +10. Scores are capped at 100 and are review-priority indicators, not probabilities. On request, the local AI assistant receives only these aggregate counts and flags, never names or IDs.
